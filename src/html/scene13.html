Tampa Bay is In Danger!

Millions of organisms live within the Tampa Bay, relying on its ecosystems for food, shelter, and survival, but rising water levels and environmental damage threaten these ecosystems.

Rising sea levels, increased flooding, and stronger storm surges are damaging habitats and making it harder for species to survive.

If these environmental threats continue, many ecosystems within Tampa Bay could be permanently altered or lost.

Two Primary Solutions

Natural barriers use plants, rocks, and land features to reduce flooding while protecting and supporting coastal ecosystems.

Sea walls are man-made structures built along shorelines to block waves, reduce flooding, and protect coastal infrastructure.

Sea Walls are not the best solution, however due to local regulations, installing these walls may be the only option.

What Can You Do?

Spreading awareness through the community.

Teaching others about the causes and effects of rising water levels.

Implementing solutions to help mitigate rising water levels.
